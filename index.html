What Our Diners Says

ထိုင်းစတိုင်ငါးငပိထောင်း

I would like to recommend one of the most popular Chinese restaurants in Yangon. The restaurant has a posh look with Chinese traditional elements infused in the decor, which makes it a unique experience. The staff is extremely helpful and courteous. For instance, when our table was overflowing with dishes, the staff promptly offered to provide a bigger table for us. The waitress, Ms Ei Shwe Zin, was very attentive and checked with us whether we were satisfied with the food. Although we had a heavy breakfast, the food at this restaurant was authentic and delicious, and we were able to finish all the dishes. I would definitely recommend this restaurant and would love to come back for more.
